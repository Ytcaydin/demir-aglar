# CrazyGames'e yükleme

1. developer.crazygames.com adresinde geliştirici hesabı aç.
2. "Submit game" → oyun türü **HTML5**.
3. Oyun dosyası: GitHub'daki son sürümün (Releases) **DemirAglar-crazygames.zip** dosyası. Zip'i açmadan yükle.
4. Kapak görselleri bu klasörde: `cover-1920x1080.png`, `cover-800x1200.png`, `cover-800x800.png`.
5. Önce "Basic Launch" ile yayına girer; o aşamada reklam yoktur. Oyun iyi giderse CrazyGames "Full Launch"a alır, reklam geliri o zaman başlar.
6. Ödeme: aylık, bakiye 100 €'yu geçince (havale veya PayPal).

Paketin içinde: CrazyGames SDK v3, ödüllü reklamlar, oyuncunun diline göre Türkçe/İngilizce, CrazyGames bulut kaydı, her zaman tam ekran düzeni (kendi tam ekran düğmemiz kapalı), yatay ekrana göre yan panel.

## Oyun sayfası metinleri (İngilizce)

**Title:** Demir Ağlar: Railway Tycoon

**Short description:** Build Turkey's railway network from the first Ottoman line in 1856 to today's high-speed trains.

**Description:**
Start in 1856 with the İzmir–Aydın line, the first railway in Anatolia, and grow it into a nationwide network by 2026. Lay track between real Turkish cities, buy trains and deliver the goods each city needs so it grows from a village into a metropolis. Live through history on decision cards — the Suez Canal, the Baghdad Railway, the proclamation of the Republic — and move from steam to diesel, electric and high-speed YHT trains across four eras. Compete with a rival company, run megaprojects, collect a city album and pass your company to the next generation.

**Controls:**
- Drag: move the map · Scroll / pinch: zoom · Right-drag / two fingers: rotate
- Click a city to select it, click a second city to plan a line
- Bottom menu: goals, company, fleet, album and shop

**Tags:** Strategy, Simulation, Trains, Tycoon, Management, Historical, 3D
